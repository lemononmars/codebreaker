# Code Breaker mascot design criteria

## Role

The mascot should act as Code Breaker's curious puzzle guide: clever without feeling smug, energetic without feeling childish, and welcoming to both Thai and international solvers. It should support the existing geometric CB mark rather than replace it.

## Non-negotiables

- Recognizable as a solid silhouette at 32 px.
- Expressive enough for success, hint, thinking, error, celebration, and loading states.
- Works in full color, one color, animation, stickers, social posts, and merchandise.
- Uses the existing charcoal/white foundation and seven-category rainbow as accents, not noise.
- Avoids hacker, hooded figure, padlock, spy, weapon, and “genius with glasses” clichés.
- Does not depend on text, a handheld prop, or tiny surface detail to communicate its idea.
- Feels friendly across ages and cultures, with an authentic connection to a Thailand-based puzzle brand.

## Weighted scorecard

| Criterion | Weight | What success looks like |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Brand and puzzle fit | 25 | Clearly belongs to Code Breaker and suggests curiosity, discovery, or pattern-solving. |
| Distinctiveness and ownership | 20 | Memorable, searchable, and difficult to confuse with a generic tech mascot. |
| Small-size readability | 15 | Face, pose, and silhouette survive favicon and reaction-sticker sizes. |
| Personality and expression | 15 | Supports a broad emotional range without changing the core design. |
| Versatility | 10 | Adapts to UI, editorial art, motion, print, and merchandise. |
| Thai and cross-cultural relevance | 10 | Has a meaningful local anchor without costume stereotypes; remains globally legible. |
| Production feasibility | 5 | Can be redrawn, rigged, and reproduced consistently at reasonable cost. |

| Candidate | Brand fit /25 | Distinct /20 | Readability /15 | Expression /15 | Versatility /10 | Cultural /10 | Production /5 | Total /100 |

| Bit — puzzle-cube robot | 23 | 12 | 12 | 14 | 9 | 3 | 3 | 76 |
| Klik — keyhole spark | 20 | 15 | 15 | 13 | 9 | 3 | 5 | 80 |
| Pang — pangolin guide | 23 | 20 | 10 | 14 | 8 | 10 | 3 | **88** |

## Decision

**Winner: Pang, the pangolin puzzle guide.**

Pang owns the best story and silhouette. The curled tail naturally forms an open C, overlapping scales suggest layered clues, and the pangolin gives a Thailand-based brand a distinctive regional anchor without relying on costume. Bit is immediately readable but belongs to a crowded robot-mascot category. Klik scales beautifully but leans toward familiar lightbulb/keyhole symbolism and feels less ownable.

Before production, simplify Pang to three scale sizes, reduce the rainbow accents to seven fixed “signal scales,” enlarge the face relative to the body, and create a one-color silhouette test. That refinement should preserve the concept while correcting its weakest score: small-size readability.

## Generated concept prompts

The three candidates were generated with the built-in image generation tool as transparent, centered, vector-like 2.5D character concepts. All prompts enforced: no text, no logo, no watermark, no scenery, no extra characters, strong thumbnail silhouette, and restrained use of the seven-category rainbow.
